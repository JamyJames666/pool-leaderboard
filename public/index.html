# Player ELO W / L Win Rate Form
How does ELO work?

ELO adjusts based on the expected result. Beating a stronger player earns more. Losing to a weaker player costs more. Everyone starts at 1000.

Even match — 1000 vs 1000
50/50 odds. Winner gets +16, loser loses -16. Standard swing.
Big favourite wins — 1300 vs 900
Expected result. Favourite gains just +4, loser drops -4. Low stakes for the better player.
Underdog wins — 900 vs 1300
Surprise! Underdog gains +28, favourite loses -28. Big rewards for upsets.
Slight edge — 1100 vs 1000
Favourite wins +13, drops -13. Slight edge means a slightly reduced reward.
