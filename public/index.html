# Player ELO W / L Win Rate Form
How does ELO work?

ELO is a rating system that adjusts based on the expected outcome of each match. Beating someone ranked much higher than you earns more points than beating someone lower. Every player starts at 1000.

Even match — 1000 vs 1000
Both have equal chances. Winner gets +16, loser gets -16. Standard swing for an even game.
Heavy favourite wins — 1300 vs 900
Expected outcome. Favourite wins +4, loser drops -4. Not much at stake when the better player wins.
Underdog wins — 900 vs 1300
Surprise result! Underdog gains +28, favourite loses -28. Big swings reward upsets.
Slightly above average — 1100 vs 1000
Favourite wins +13, drops -13. Slight edge means slightly reduced reward for the expected winner.
